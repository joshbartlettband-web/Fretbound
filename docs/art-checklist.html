Fretbound art shoot

Painted backdrops for the title screen, and a proper world map for backstage. Tap a card's button to copy its whole prompt, paste it into Gemini, and tick it off when you have an image you like.

How it works

  1. Tap Copy prompt, then paste it into Gemini. Ask for two or three versions and keep the best.
  2. If something is off (text, a road, the horizon too high), use the fix lines at the bottom.
  3. Download your favourite and tick its box here. Your ticks are saved on this phone.
  4. Send the images to me in one message and say which is which, for example "1 is North America, 2 is Africa". I'll remove the watermark, line up the horizon, crisp up the pixels and match the colours, then show you before and after.
